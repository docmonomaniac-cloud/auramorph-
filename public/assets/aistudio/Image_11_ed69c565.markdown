## Brand & Style

This design system defines a high-performance, AI-driven fitness transformation interface tailored for the modern Indian fitness ecosystem. The aesthetic fuses the discipline of sports science with the sleek clarity of high-end biometric software.

### Visual Character
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep, velvety charcoal canvases layered with dark glass cards create an immersive, low-distraction dark mode.
- **Electric Vitality:** High-octane lime and emerald neon accents command attention, visualizing metabolic momentum, bio-feedback, and AI execution.
- **Scientific Cleanliness:** Balanced layouts, structured tabular metrics, and crisp sans-serif typography deliver an interface that feels calibrated and authoritative without intimidating beginners.
- **Tactile Modernism:** Frosted glassy surfaces, hairline emerald stroke highlights, and concentrated neon glows replicate the feel of futuristic physical performance equipment.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark-mode optimization, maximum contrast ratios, and controlled bursts of luminescence.

### Base Canvases
- **Background Root (`#0A0D0E`):** Pure foundational charcoal surface for application frames and root views.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#111618`):** Base container fill for modules, cards, and bottom sheets.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#182022`):** Elevated cards, interactive inputs, and hover states.

### Accents & Illumination
- **Primary Electric Lime (`#A3E635`):** Reserved for core calls-to-action, primary bio-stats, active streak badges, and focus triggers.
- **Secondary Emerald (`#10B981`):** Applied to success states, completion bars, recovery indicators, and subtle translucent border glows.
- **Tertiary Neon Mint (`#4ADE80`):** Secondary interactive elements, macro-nutrient charts, and AI coach conversational highlights.

### Typography & Neutrals
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp high-contrast white for headers and primary values.
- **Text Muted Slate (`#94A3B8`):** Body typography, contextual captions, and supporting metric labels.
- **Text Dimmed (`#64748B`):** Inactive states, structural timestamps, and ghosted placeholders.
- **Border Subtle (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`): Hairline structural strokes that outline elevated glassmorphic boundaries.

## Typography

The typography couples the technological character of `Space Grotesk` with the legibility of `Inter`. 

- **Display & Headings:** Set in `Space Grotesk`. Its mechanical geometric proportions reflect athletic performance engineering and precise biometric measurement.
- **Body & Longform:** Set in `Inter`. Designed for legibility across variable nutrition tracking tables, diet breakdowns, and dynamic workout regimes.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Large numerical readouts use `Space Grotesk` with tabular figures enabled to ensure linear alignment during active rep counts and caloric calculations.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8px base rhythm governs spatial distribution. 

### Grid Configuration
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Vertical stack prioritizes the immediate daily target and active schedule.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins, enabling split views for workout tracking alongside metabolic telemetry.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxing at 1440px wide with `3rem` margins. Three-column dashboard: sidebar navigation, central performance stream, and dedicated AI coach summary.

### Spacing Philosophy
Dense telemetry tiles leverage compact paddings (`space-sm` and `space-md`) to ensure critical fitness stats remain visible above the fold, while hero transformation sequences and progress photos utilize expansive paddings (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent planes, dark glassmorphism, and neon radiance rather than traditional drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid `#0A0D0E`.
- **Tier 1 (Glass Container):** Background `rgba(17, 22, 24, 0.75)` with `16px` backdrop-blur and a hairline `1px` border of `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Overlay):** Background `rgba(24, 32, 34, 0.85)` with `24px` backdrop-blur and a subtle top-edge highlight of `rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.20)`.

### Bio-Luminescent Radiance
Interactive and active elements emit diffuse color glows:
- **Active State Glow:** `box-shadow: 0px 0px 24px -4px rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.25)`.
- **Success & Progression Glow:** `box-shadow: 0px 0px 28px -6px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a calibrated modern curvature (`roundedness: 2`):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Secondary Buttons):** `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius, signaling structural precision.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** `1rem` (16px) corner radius, creating clear visual containment for nutrition logs and workout stats.
- **Floating Modals & Hero Containers:** `1.5rem` (24px) corner radius to soften high-density metrics.
- **Indicators & Action Triggers:** Full-pill radiuses for status badges, workout timers, and primary action CTAs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Log Workout, Generate Plan):** Solid `#A3E635` background with `#0A0D0E` typography (`Space Grotesk`, medium weight). Outer glow of `0 0 20px rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.3)`. On hover, scale 1.02 with increased glow intensity.
- **Secondary (Rest Day, Custom Swap):** Translucent fill `rgba(24, 32, 34, 0.8)` with a 1px border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)` and `#F8FAFC` typography.
- **Ghost/Destructive:** Transparent background with `#64748B` text, transitioning to red-accented borders strictly on destructive confirms.

### Cards & Glass Panels
- **Telemetry Card:** Backed by `rgba(17, 22, 24, 0.8)` frosted glass, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bordered by hairline `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`. Top interior padding carries a 1px directional highlight gradient fading from `#A3E635` (at 10% opacity) to transparent.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields (Reps, Weight, Search):** Background `#111618`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)`, text `#F8FAFC`. On focus, border transitions to `#A3E635` with an ambient glow of `0 0 10px rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.2)`.
- **Checkboxes & Segment Controls:** Custom rounded squares (6px) with an unchecked border of `#64748B`. Checked state fills with `#A3E635` and displays a `#0A0D0E` checkmark.

### Badges & Nutrition Chips
- **Macro & Metric Chips:** Background `rgba(24, 32, 34, 0.9)`, border `1px solid rgba(74, 222, 128, 0.2)`. Key metrics (Protein, Calories, Pace) prefix with a 6px circular neon dot indicator.

### Specialized Fitness Patterns
- **AI Coach Dialogue Cards:** Subtle gradient outline running from `#A3E635` down to `#10B981` at 30% opacity, highlighting AI recommendations against deep charcoal backgrounds.
- **Workout Progression Bar:** Track uses `#182022`, while the completed segment features a linear gradient of `#10B981` to `#A3E635` ending in an animated glowing pulse head.
